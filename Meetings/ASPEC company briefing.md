### 3. How they run projects — accreditations and systems

**Health & safety** ([H&S page](https://www.aspecconstruction.co.nz/health-and-safety/)):
- **SiteWise Gold**
- **Impac Prequal** certification
- **Tōtika** accreditation
- Framework built on the **Health and Safety at Work Act 2015**; hazard ID/assessment, incident investigation, H&S Committees, annual strategic and operational H&S objectives, **regular internal and external audits**
- **MATES in Construction** partner; EAP for all staff

**Environmental / sustainability** ([Sustainability page](https://www.aspecconstruction.co.nz/aspec-sustainability/), [Toitū register](https://www.toitu.co.nz/members/aspec-construction-limited/)):
- **Toitū Enviromark Gold** — certificate 16122025, issued **16 Dec 2025**, valid to 15 Dec 2026. Aligned to ISO 14001:2015
- **Toitū Carbonreduce** (ISO 14064-1), and they are pulling supply-chain emissions data from subbies
- **ISO 14001 EMS journey began 2025**
- Delivered **Green Star 4–6 star** projects and **one Living Building Challenge** project (Franklin Amenities; Metlifecare Pohutakawa Landing)

**Quality**
- 🔴 **No public evidence of ISO 9001 or any named quality management system.** Their values page (Excellence, Collaboration, Accountability, Reliability, Sustainability) contains **no** QA process, defect protocol or compliance system detail. The construction division page only claims *"latest construction techniques, advanced technology, and strong management systems"* with nothing specific behind it. **This is a genuine gap in their public posture — and it is exactly the space Soterra sells into.**

**Master Builders / NZ Certified Builders**
- 🔴 **NOT VERIFIED.** I found no membership listing for either body. They have won **NZ Commercial Project Awards** (owned by the Registered Master Builders Association) — Gold 2020, Silver 2018 — but the awards site states entry is *"open to all NZ based commercial construction project partners"*, so an award does not prove membership. **Do not claim they are Master Builders members.**

**Technology**
- **BIM is confirmed** — their Old Choral Hall page describes *"integration of new structural steel and services via BIM modeling"*.
- 🔴 **No public evidence of Procore, Aconex, Autodesk Construction Cloud, Trimble, or any named QA/defects/site-diary platform.** I searched specifically and found nothing. **This is an open question to ask the PM, not something to assume either way.**

### 5. Pain-point read for the demo — clearly labelled as inference

**No public statement by ASPEC about rework, defects, consent delays or inspection failures exists.** I searched for it directly and found nothing. Everything in this section is **my inference from what they build**, not a documented ASPEC complaint. Frame it in the meeting as a question, not a diagnosis.

What the evidence structurally implies:

1. **Heritage + seismic retrofit is a core line of business** (Old Choral Hall, Dio Chapel & St Barnabas, Auckland Museum, Ascot Hospital facade, Baradene, Remuera Rail Station). Existing-building work means constant reference to specific structural details, producer statements, Heritage NZ constraints and Category 1 obligations — a plan-lookup-heavy environment where "what does the detail say" is asked dozens of times a day.
2. **Coordination volume is extreme.** 1,304 contractors and 50+ consultants on one job; 30 Aspec site managers and 150 peak workers on another. Consistency of answers across that many people is a real, measurable problem — and a strong angle for cited answers everyone can check.
3. **They work in live, occupied buildings** — operating schools, an operating retirement village (Parkside was staged around residents), hospitals, a museum. Staged handovers, holiday-window programmes (St Mary's Stage 2 is explicitly scheduled into 2026/27 school holidays). Compressed windows make an inspection failure or a return visit disproportionately expensive. **This is your sharpest pre-inspection-check pitch.**
4. **Quality is their public claim but not their public system.** Excellence, Reliability, "highest quality standards" appear everywhere; a named QMS appears nowhere. They have Gold-tier H&S (SiteWise, Tōtika, Impac) and Gold-tier environmental (Toitū) but no visible quality equivalent. **Position Soterra as the missing third leg, in the same "certified, auditable, evidence-backed" language they already use for H&S and environmental.**
5. **They already do the digital lift.** BIM on Old Choral Hall, supply-chain carbon data capture from subbies, external audits. They are not technophobic — but nothing found suggests a Procore-class platform is already in place. **Ask the PM what they run today before positioning against anything.**

**Demo tailoring recommendation:** lead with a **school or heritage/seismic scenario**, not a house. An education-campus example in a live-term environment, or a heritage seismic detail with a cited answer, will land far harder than generic residential. Their PMs' day looks like: a Category 1 heritage constraint, a structural steel detail, a school holiday window, and 40 subbies who all need the same answer.

- The 2026 Building People Awards on 14 August is four days after the meeting, and Jeff Robertson is a director and a finalist. This is a strong rapport hook, but avoid implying the outcome is known.
