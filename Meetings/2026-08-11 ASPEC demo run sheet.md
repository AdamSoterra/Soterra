## THE THREE THINGS MOST LIKELY TO GO WRONG

**1. The assistant claims it has a manufacturer it cannot actually search.**
It is told it holds 14 brands. This account holds three: GIB, BOSS Fire, Kingspan Thermakraft. If he asks about James Hardie, Allproof, Resene, Rondo, Ryanfire, ColorSteel, Concrete NZ, Roofing Industries, Dimond or APL, it will say it has them and then come back with nothing.

> **Recovery:** "Good catch. That account's only got three manufacturers loaded, and it's over-promising, which I'd rather it didn't do. The brands you actually use are the ones we load for you. Which ones are on your jobs most? Write them down and I'll tell you exactly where each one sits on permission."
>
> Turn it into discovery. It is a corpus question, not a product failure, and it makes the "plans govern, specified product wins" argument for you.

**2. A citation card opens blank or fails.**
BOSS Fire and Kingspan pages render live, on the spot. Only 119 of 823 GIB pages are pre-rendered.

> **Recovery:** "That one's rendering the page live off the source PDF, give it a second." Wait 3 seconds. If still blank: close it, say "I'll show you that one on a GIB page, they're cached and instant" and immediately reopen a GIB citation from Q2. Do not sit staring at a blank card. Move within five seconds.

**3. The QA check or safety plan errors out, or hangs past 60 seconds.**
Usually credit or rate limiting. It surfaces as "The assistant couldn't be reached just now."

> **Recovery:** "That's our API throttling, not the product. It regenerates." Hit it once more. **If the second attempt fails, stop trying.** Say: "I'll send you the generated check by lunchtime today so you can see the real output." Then move straight to the RFI section, which is plain text generation and the least likely thing in the app to break. Never let a spinner run twice in front of a prospect.

**Also possible, lower stakes:**
- He asks about the drawings, past inspection failures, or "what do we keep failing on". Both are empty. Recovery: "Nothing in there yet, that account's a day old. That's exactly what the two week trial fills in, and your inspection history is a one-off $200 to turn into insights."
- A determination question returns nothing. Recovery: retype it in two or three words. Long questions return zero. Do not retype the same long sentence.
