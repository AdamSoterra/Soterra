Fire collar type at PVC waste penetrations through the GBTLAB 60D wall, Level 3

📍 Level 3, Units 3.02 to 3.05, intertenancy wallRaised Tue 1 Sep 2026
Question
Detail 7 on A-311 calls up a fire collar at the 100mm uPVC waste where it passes through the 60/60/60 intertenancy wall, but no product or system is specified. The GBTLAB 60D manual lists a tested penetration schedule. Can you confirm which collar and tested system we should use here, and whether the same applies to the 50mm waste in the same wall? We want this locked before the second layer of lining goes on.
Northgate Construction's proposed solution
Use a tested intumescent fire collar from the 60D wall system's penetration schedule, fitted both sides of the wall and fixed back to the framing, with the system's fire-rated sealant. Same collar family in the smaller size for the 50mm.
