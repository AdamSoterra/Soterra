What Soterra is

Your company's private AI assistant for the whole job.

🔍
Before the job

Catch clashes before you build.

It reads your specs, PS1s and drawings and flags what does not line up, before it becomes an issue on site.
✅
Quality assurance

The QA checks that matter on this job.

As New Zealand shifts more liability onto main contractors, a generic sheet will not protect you. Built from this job's specs, plans and Code, weighted by what your crew fails most.
💬
During the job

Answers cited to the exact page.

Ask any detail and get the answer from whichever source governs, your plans, CIs and CANs, the Code, a Standard or the manufacturer, linked to the page.
🦺
Health & Safety

Site-specific H&S, cited to WorkSafe.

It builds your H&S plan from this job's real hazards, and gives the controls for the task in front of you, cited to WorkSafe.
📅
Programme

Check your programme against the job.

Upload your build programme and Soterra checks it against the scope, flagging gaps, out-of-order work and missing inspection hold-points.
📊
Track & prove

RFIs, defects and QA, all tracked.

Every RFI, defect and QA item from raised to signed off. See what is open, where it sits, and what keeps coming back.
🔒 Your data is yours

Sealed to your company. Nobody else can reach it.

It never trains anyone else's assistant and never lands in a shared pool. A separate, access-gated space per project. Your projects stay your competitive edge.
