How it works behind the scenes

  • Sender: each project gets its own address on send.soterra.co.nz (e.g. kauri-tower@send.soterra.co.nz). Using a subdomain means your Microsoft 365 mail is completely untouched.
  • Replies: Reply-To is the person who pressed Send, so answers land in their normal inbox. No new inbox to check. (Auto-capturing replies into the thread is the fast-follow.)
  • Recorded: every send stores recipient, cc, subject, body, attachments and the outbound message id, linked to the flag / RFI / inspection item it belongs to. That record is what the analytics and the EOT pack read.
  • Fonts: emails render in Arial on purpose. Gmail and Outlook strip web fonts, so the design is built to look right without DM Sans rather than degrade.
  • Provider: Resend (transactional email service). You create the account, I wire everything: 3 DNS records on send.soterra.co.nz, verified, then every send goes through it.
