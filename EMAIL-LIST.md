## Say these things in every email

1. **Verbatim, cited by document name and page, never paraphrased** in a way that could misstate their spec, and always pointing at their current published version.
2. **Version-pinned with a refresh commitment.** Their systems are CodeMark-certified, which obliges installation per the *current* manual — staleness is the objection they'll actually raise.
3. **You are not asking for third-party pages.** Any embedded BRANZ Appraisal or Standards NZ content is excluded, because it isn't theirs to grant. Naming this before they do is what separates a request that gets answered from one that gets forwarded to legal.
4. **Why it serves them:** incorrect installation is their warranty and reputation cost. This puts their spec in front of the installer at the moment of installation.
